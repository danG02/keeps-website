How it works

Three quiet steps between trying something and remembering it.

  1. 1

    Add what you tried

    Name, brand, collection, a photo if you like — place it on your shelf.

  2. 2

    Leave a verdict

    Ask yourself: would future you buy this again? From Regret to Favorite.

  3. 3

    Find it again

    Browse Favorites, Buy again, or any collection when you need the answer.
